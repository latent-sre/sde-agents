## Measured results — and the missed target

All local figures are back-to-back same-machine pairs (this machine's suite variance is ±10s;
unpaired comparisons are noise):

**The spec's ~60 s suite target was missed and reported, not amended.** Root cause: cProfile's
per-run fraction was right (~60% adapter-check), but suite wall time is dominated by the
869-file repository copy plus cleanup through Defender (~0.9 s/test); CI's ubuntu leg runs the

suite to ~30–38 s measured on 2026-08-09 — credited to that PR, not to this round.

## Lessons routed

- **Profiler fractions ≠ wall attribution.** A per-run cProfile share does not predict suite
  wall time when a per-test fixed cost (file-copy through a filesystem filter) dominates; the
  instrument for an optimization claim is a controlled same-state A/B on the same machine. This
  is now enforceable prose: the proportionality rule's "optimization without a paired
  same-machine measurement is a defect" clause (AGENTS.md, PR #90).
- **Pull before measuring.** The round briefly diagnosed "broken main" from a local clone ten
  commits behind origin (failures already fixed upstream by #84/#87). A measurement session
  starts by proving the tree is current.
- Small-item candidate noted in the round PR and imported at this closeout as SMALL-006: the
  AGENTS.md Map has no row for `scripts/ledger_drift.py` (pre-existing gap).
