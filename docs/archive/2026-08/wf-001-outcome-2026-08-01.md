## Lessons (for the next round's process, not this repo's rules)

- **Subagent background-waits stall.** Two sonnet implementers parked on their own
  background-task notifications (Tasks 3 and 6; hours lost once). Dispatch prompts now forbid
  background waits for gates, and the controller arms a commit-appearance watchdog per task.
- **Harnesses must pin their own model.** Probe sessions inherited the operator's new
  Fable/xhigh default until killed and re-run with `ANTHROPIC_MODEL=sonnet`. Candidate
  hardening: pin inside `probe_plugin.py` itself rather than per-invocation.
- **Windows CRLF is a workflow-killer.** `Path.write_text()` without `newline="\n"` produced a
  script the Workflow tool rejected as containing hidden control characters — masquerading as
  "hooks never fire in workflows." The probe now writes LF explicitly and its launch oracle
  requires the tool's own acknowledgment (the obvious oracle was vacuously green).
- **The pilot's economics justify the pattern narrowly.** ~$4/11 min per review-pipeline run is
  acceptable for gate-bearing reviews, not for casual use; conversion of further pipelines waits
  for GRAPH-004's trigger, not enthusiasm.
