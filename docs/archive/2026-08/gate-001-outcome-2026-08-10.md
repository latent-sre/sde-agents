## Process record — six reviews and a step-back

Deep-review criticals ran 5 → 2 → 1 → 1 → 1 → 1 across six rounds (~1.5M review tokens);
rounds 1–2 found real implementation defects, rounds 3–6 each found defects in the previous
round's fix sentences — three successive verifier-continuation designs died this way. The
operator's step-back ended the loop: strip the mechanism, fix the infrastructure, and encode
the **review-loop stop rule** now in AGENTS.md (ledger `lc_8223961a`): at most two static
rounds on prose-behavior changes; later criticals route to a live behavioral run or an
executed verification pass. The final closing instruments were exactly those: a live contract
run and read field probes.
