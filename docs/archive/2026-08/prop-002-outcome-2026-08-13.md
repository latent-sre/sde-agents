# PROP-002 outcome — right-sizing skill-level enterprise ceremony (2026-08-13)

**Status:** closed. Historical outcome record; not a task list. The roadmap item is retired to this
file.

**What the round was.** A four-scanner proportionality sweep of all 20 skills, hunting enterprise
gates and ceremony that a single-operator home lab pays for on every session without the
organizational scale that earns them. The full findings, KEEP lists, corrections, and the
finding-by-finding backlog disposition live in
[`prop-002-scan-findings-2026-08-13.md`](prop-002-scan-findings-2026-08-13.md); this record is what
the round decided and what it is worth remembering.

## What landed

**Five HIGH clusters (PR #132, merged 2026-08-13).** `self-improve-loop`'s promotion gate went from
seven enumerated gates to four; `sre-tool`'s run-state control-plane mandate narrowed from "any
multi-agent run" to safety-critical or unattended, and its Phase 4 chain-of-custody procedure became
commit-or-inconclusive; `service-onboard` stopped restating `runbook`'s 15-slot contract eleven
lines from the link to the skill that owns it; `backend-craft` gained a definition of "published"
keyed to deployment atomicity; `lab-incident` stopped owing a postmortem for every near-miss.

**Six MEDIUM findings (`eb53758`).** Each conditions a mandate on the scale that pays for it rather
than removing a mechanism: workflow linting graduates from local to CI when someone else can break
a workflow, SBOM and provenance key to the artifact leaving the lab, contract tests key to a
consumer not deployed in the same commit, expand→migrate→contract keys to a migration that cannot
stop its readers, the circuit breaker keys to volume that can hurt something, the interface-contract
artifact keys to more than one builder writing against the interface, and `frontend-craft` stopped
instructing sessions to invent unrequested features to fill an empty-looking page.

**The rest of the backlog, dispositioned rather than worked.** Four overturned, six deferred to a
named item that already owns a paid run on the same file, five dropped with reasons, and ~30 LOWs
dropped as one batch under a shared reason.

## What is worth remembering

**1. The largest backlog item was contraindicated by evidence another item had already bought.**
The scan's biggest single recommendation was relocating `runbook`'s 42-line propose grammar out of
the always-loaded body into `references/`. It cannot work: `runbook-disposition-propose` runs as a
skill-only session with no `Read`, so a reference file is unreachable from exactly the session that
must obey the grammar. LEARN-002's 2026-08-10 calibration had already measured this failure in the
other direction — a load-bearing literal living only in `references/` kept three contracts at 0/3,
and the chosen fix was moving the literal *into* the body. The same round considered and rejected
granting `Read` to the cases, because it "would leave every other skill-only caller in the same
position". The generalizable rule: **in a skill whose behavior is graded by tool-restricted
sessions, "move it to a reference" is a reachability change, not a formatting change.** Relocation
is only free for text no restricted session needs.

**2. Findings that target an untrusted LLM worker read as org ceremony and are not.** Three separate
findings — `run_state.py`'s control plane (overturned during the HIGH round), the plan-file gate
register, and the interface contract's named owner — all describe controls whose adversary is a
model worker claiming an approval or a completion it was never given. Headcount is the wrong axis
for those; they are real at any size. A proportionality scan will keep surfacing them, because they
look identical in prose to the sign-off registers that genuinely are ceremony. The distinguishing
question is *who is the control defending against* — a colleague you can just ask, or a worker whose
claims you cannot verify.

**3. Sequencing beat editing.** Six findings sit in files that LEARN-002, LADDER-001, or LANE-001
will already be re-measuring with paid sessions. Trimming them here would have bought a second
measurement to prove a change the first one was going to cover, and — for `eng-ladder` and
`onboarding-map` — would have moved bytes out from under a 'before' capture an operator is about to
purchase. Deferring is the proportionate call, and it is only honest because each deferral names
the item that carries it.

**4. A prose round can close without buying a run, if it says so.** Every edit in the closeout was
chosen to be provable by the deterministic gates alone: conditioning a mandate or deleting a
restatement in text no behavioral contract grades. That is a real but narrow claim — consistency,
not measured session behavior — and the scan record states it in those terms rather than implying
the trims were measured.

**5. The scan's own tiering held up better than its remedies.** Of the five HIGH clusters, all five
were real and all five shipped. Of the remedies proposed for them, two were wrong in a way only
liveness evidence could show (the release-retest lifecycle was field-earned, not ceremony; the
runbook relocation would break a contract). A scanner reading prose at rest can identify
disproportionate cost accurately and still propose a fix that the running system refutes — which is
why the KEEP lists and the Corrections section, not the finding list, are the parts of that record
with the longest useful life.

## Verification

Deterministic gates only, deliberately, per point 4 above: `scripts/validate_fleet.py` clean;
`scripts/run_tests.py` green (837 tests across 33 modules); regenerated host adapters byte-identical
to canonical sources; `claude plugin validate . --strict` passing. The HIGH round additionally
carried paired behavioral evidence (`sre-tool` durable-state 3/3 before == 3/3 at final head) and a
deep-review `merge-with-nits` with zero confirmed criticals, both recorded in the scan findings.

## What this round did not do

- No description was edited, so no routing run is owed and none was bought.
- No mechanism was removed. Every trim conditions, delegates, or deletes a restatement; the learning
  ledger, run-state control plane, and release-retest lifecycle are untouched.
- The deferred findings are not closed. Six of them are carried by LEARN-002, LADDER-001, and
  LANE-001, and are listed in the scan record's disposition section with the item that owns each.
